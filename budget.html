How Budget Quest works

  1. Set your goal: enter what you are saving for and how much it costs.
  2. Add money in: choose “Money in” for allowance, gifts, or other money you receive.
  3. Log money spent: choose “Money spent” whenever you buy something. Your available balance updates automatically.
  4. Plan ahead: add upcoming charges with a due date, then check them off when they are paid.
  5. Check your progress: use the balance, activity list, and savings bar to see where your money went and how much is left.
